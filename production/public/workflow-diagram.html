2 Detailed Spine — sab kuch record_payment() pe milta hai

Poore product ka dil ek hi function hai. 3 alag payment raaste isi pe aakar milte hain, aur ek hi transaction mein 6 cheezein hoti hain.

flowchart TD
    A["Lead · stage=new"]:::lead --> B["contact → demo
(sales baat karta hai)"]:::lead
    B --> T["🎁 Free Trial
stage=trial · +3 reminder tasks"]:::lead
    B --> Q["📄 Quote ban gaya
status=sent · payment=awaiting"]:::quote
    T --> Q

    Q --> ACC["Customer ne quote accept kiya
/quote/[id]/accept"]:::quote
    Q --> PAYUI["Andar se: Record Payment dialog"]:::pay
    Q --> CHK["Public checkout /buy
→ Razorpay order"]:::pay
    ACC --> PAYUI
    ACC --> CHK

    CHK -->|live: webhook| RP
    CHK -->|keys nahi: simulation| RP
    PAYUI --> RP

    RP{{"💥 record_payment() RPC
atomic · 1 transaction"}}:::hub

    RP --> C1["Lead → Customer ban gaya
lead.stage = WON 🎉"]:::done
    RP --> C2["Receipt Voucher issue
(GST compliant)"]:::done
    RP --> C3["payments ledger entry"]:::done
    RP --> C4["Subscription ACTIVE
renewal_date = +1 saal"]:::sub
    RP --> C5["Invoice PAID mark
(agar invoice juda ho)"]:::done

    C4 --> SUB["📦 Subscription chal rahi hai"]:::sub
    SUB --> REN["⏰ Renewals cron roz 9 baje"]:::cron
    REN --> CAD["Reminder cadence T-15 → grace
notice→reminder 1-4→final"]:::cron
    CAD --> RQ["Renewal quote auto-bana + email"]:::quote
    RQ -->|customer pays| RP
    CAD -->|grace khatam, unpaid| SUSP["⛔ Auto-suspend
sub=paused"]:::lost

    RP -. "PLANNED (abhi nahi bana)" .-> GEN["generate_invoice / refund_payment RPC"]:::plan
