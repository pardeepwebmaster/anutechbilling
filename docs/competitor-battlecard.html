Quick win / lose guide

You WIN when the reseller…
  • Wants ONE tool for lead→quote→GST invoice→renew→collect, not 4 stitched together
  • Is bleeding on manual per-seat margin reconciliation & missed renewals
  • Needs India GST/IRP/TDS/UPI/WhatsApp out-of-the-box at SMB price
  • Is an SMB that finds RackNap/global tools too enterprise/expensive
You LOSE when the reseller…
  • Already "copes fine" on Zoho/Tally + spreadsheets (the real enemy = inertia)
  • Is an enterprise/distributor who needs RackNap's depth & is fine with quote pricing
  • Needs deep auto-provisioning into Partner Center / Google today (your 🟡 gap)
  • Trusts an incumbent brand (Zoho/Tally) over a brand-new 0-customer product

RackNap Closest direct threat

Profile: India-origin (ZNet Technologies, Jaipur; now under In Time Tec). Cloud service-delivery & business-automation platform: ordering → billing → provisioning → renewals. Multi-currency INR + GST, 2-tier reseller model, covers M365/Azure + Google Workspace.
Pricing: Quote-only — no public SMB self-serve tier.
Target: Enterprises, distributors, telcos, large CSPs.
Where they win
  • Genuinely does GST + INR + renewals + 2-tier reseller + provisioning — the full stack
  • Mature, enterprise-grade, India-origin credibility
  • Deep provisioning you don't have yet
Where you win
  • SMB self-serve & affordable — they're quote-only enterprise/telco
  • Built for the small reseller's daily workflow, not distributor scale
  • Faster, simpler onboarding; modern UX; WhatsApp-first India SMB feel
Talk track: "RackNap is excellent if you're a distributor or telco running thousands of seats with a sales team to handle their quote-based onboarding. ResellerOS is built for you — the focused Indian reseller who wants to be live today at a price that fits SMB margins, without an enterprise contract."

Landmine questions to ask a prospect

  • "Did you get a transparent monthly price, or a custom enterprise quote?"
  • "How long was onboarding — could you self-serve, or did it need their team?"

Zoho Biggest substitute (inertia)

Profile: Chennai. The default India SMB suite, GST Suvidha Provider, 100M+ users. Not one product — a stitched bundle: CRM + Invoice/Billing + Books.
Pricing (excl. GST): Zoho CRM ₹800–1,400/user/mo · Invoice free · Billing ₹3,950–7,900/mo · Books ₹899–9,999/mo · Zoho One ₹1,250/emp or ₹3,500/user/mo.
Where they win
  • Ticks every horizontal box: CRM, GST+IRP e-invoice, subscriptions, TDS, bank-rec, portal, WhatsApp
  • India-tax-perfect, trusted brand, cheap, single login (Zoho One)
  • Resellers can already "limp along" on it
Where you win
  • Zoho doesn't know what a Google/MS seat is — no per-seat cost-vs-sell margin
  • No provisioning, no "lead→quote→provision→renew" as one cloud-seat lifecycle
  • No partner/distributor hierarchy; reseller re-keys seats & computes margin in spreadsheets
  • You = purpose-built; Zoho = horizontal glue the reseller must assemble & maintain
Talk track: "Zoho is great horizontal software — but it treats a Google Workspace seat like any product line. You still re-key seat counts, track cost-vs-sell margin in Excel, and chase renewals manually. ResellerOS does that cloud-seat lifecycle automatically, with the same GST compliance Zoho gives you."

Landmine questions

  • "How do you track margin per Google/M365 SKU today — is it a monthly spreadsheet?"
  • "How many tools/logins is your lead-to-renewal flow spread across?"
  • "How do you catch a renewal before the customer's service lapses?"

Global CSP platforms — Pax8 · CloudCockpit · AppXite · AppDirect · CloudBlue · TD SYNNEX Stellr · Sherweb

Profile: US/EU/Canada marketplace & CSP-billing engines for Western MSPs/distributors. Do reseller ops (catalog, provisioning, billing, renewals, white-label) very well.
Pricing: AppDirect $799–1,499/mo + GMV fees; Pax8/Sherweb/CloudBlue margin-based, distributor-scale; CloudCockpit transaction-based. All in USD/EUR economics.
India: Pax8 not in India (ANZ-first for Google, 2026). None bill in INR.
Where they win
  • Best-in-class catalog breadth, provisioning automation, storefronts
  • Deep vendor alliances (Microsoft, Google, AWS)
Where you win
  • Zero India compliance: no GST invoice, no IRP/IRN/QR e-invoice, no INR, no Razorpay/UPI, no TDS, no WhatsApp
  • Priced for US/EU margins — prohibitive for an Indian SMB reseller
  • The reseller still bolts on Tally/Zoho → fragmented workflow you unify
Talk track: "Pax8 and friends are world-class — for an American MSP. None of them issues an Indian GST tax invoice, pushes to the IRP for an IRN, bills in INR, or collects on UPI. You'd still need Tally on top. ResellerOS is that whole stack, India-native, in one tool."

⚠️ Threat to watch: if the India cloud market grows, Pax8 / CloudCockpit could localise. Your moat = depth & speed of India GST/IRP/UPI/WhatsApp compliance they'd have to rebuild.

TallyPrime · Refrens · Vyapar · myBillBook · Chargebee

TallyPrime (Bengaluru) — the accounting/GST ledger every Indian SMB's CA already uses. ₹22,500–67,500 perpetual or ₹750–2,250/mo. Gold-standard accounting, GST returns, e-invoice, bank-rec, WhatsApp share. No CRM, no subscriptions, no provisioning, no per-seat margin, no portal. → You feed Tally, you don't replace it.
Refrens — cheap freelancer/agency all-in-one. Free–₹100/mo. GST+IRN, light CRM, WhatsApp reminders. The low-end nipper — but service-generic, no reseller/seat/renewal model.
Vyapar / myBillBook — mobile GST billing for kirana/retail/traders. ₹217/mo–₹1,099/yr. Not B2B-cloud-reseller suited. Not real substitutes.
Chargebee (India-born, now global) — subscription billing for companies selling their own SaaS, not third-party seat reselling. Free→~$599/mo. Wrong buyer. Not a competitor.
Talk track: "Keep Tally — it's your accountant's ledger. ResellerOS sits in front of it: it runs your reseller sales, GST invoicing, margin and renewals, then hands clean data to Tally. We're the front-of-house; Tally's the back office."

MSP / PSA — ConnectWise · HaloPSA · Atera · Kaseya/Autotask · SuperOps · Syncro

Profile: Platforms to run managed-services IT businesses (ticketing, RMM device-monitoring, contracts, billing). SuperOps is India-founded (Chennai) but targets global MSPs.
Pricing: $35–219 per technician/mo (per-tech model). ConnectWise $9K–85K/yr.
Where they win
  • Mature ticketing, RMM, SLA, contract management for IT services firms
Where you win
  • Wrong economics: they bill per technician; a license reseller's cost driver is seats × upstream cost
  • RMM/device-monitoring is irrelevant — a Workspace reseller has no devices to monitor
  • No Indian GST/IRP/TDS/Razorpay/WhatsApp
Talk track: "PSA tools are for IT services firms managing devices and tickets, priced per technician. You don't monitor servers — you resell cloud seats. ResellerOS is shaped around seats, margin, renewals and GST, not technicians and tickets."

⭐ ResellerOS — your position

The one-line moat: the only tool aiming at the intersection no one occupies — the Indian cloud-reseller lifecycle (lead→quote→provision→renew, per-seat margin, partner hierarchy) + the full Indian tax/payments stack (GST, IRP e-invoice, TDS, Razorpay/UPI, WhatsApp) — at an SMB-affordable INR price.
Differentiators (defend these)
  • India-GST-native AND reseller-margin-aware AND renewal-automated — together
  • SMB self-serve & affordable (vs RackNap enterprise/quote, global tools' USD pricing)
  • Purpose-built workflow vs Zoho's horizontal glue
  • WhatsApp-first, UPI-first — built for how Indian SMBs actually operate
Honest gaps to close
  • 0 paying customers — biggest risk is adoption/inertia, not features
  • Auto-provisioning into Partner Center / Google = 🟡 not built (RackNap/global have it)
  • Money-spine not yet launch-ready (see MONEY-FLOW-TEST-MATRIX.md)
  • Brand-new vs trusted incumbents (Zoho/Tally)

Strategic read

  • Market shape: genuine white space at the SMB tier — no exact India-native direct competitor.
  • Real enemy = inertia, not a rival. Resellers cope "well enough" on Zoho/Tally + spreadsheets. The battle is behaviour change.
  • Benchmark explicitly against: RackNap (move-up-market risk) & Zoho Billing (move-adjacent risk).
  • Wedge message: "Stop stitching Zoho + Tally + spreadsheets + reminders. One India-native tool from lead to renewal."
